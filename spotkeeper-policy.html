Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SpotKeeper handles information. SpotKeeper is designed as an offline-first private place diary where your saved places, notes, ratings, photos, and map links stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

1. Overview

SpotKeeper is developed by Stone Forze as a private offline-first Android app for saving personal place memories. The app helps users save places such as cafes, restaurants, parks, shops, doctors, clinics, service centers, travel spots, and other useful locations.

SpotKeeper is not a social network, public review platform, public map directory, or location tracking app. The app is designed for private personal use.

Core privacy principle: Your saved places are intended to remain on your device. SpotKeeper does not require login, account creation, or a backend server for its core features.

2. Information Stored by the App

SpotKeeper may store the information you manually add inside the app. This information is stored locally on your device to make the app work offline.

  • Place name
  • Place category
  • Personal rating
  • Personal notes
  • Photo selected by you
  • Map link pasted by you
  • Optional address or tags if added
  • Favorite status
  • Created and updated timestamps

This information is used only to display, search, edit, filter, export, or manage your saved places inside the app.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

SpotKeeper is designed to avoid unnecessary data collection. For the core app experience, we do not collect or upload your personal place diary data to our servers.

  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not require login.
  • We do not collect your saved place names.
  • We do not collect your personal notes.
  • We do not collect your saved photos.
  • We do not collect your pasted map links.
  • We do not track your background location.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

4. App Permissions

SpotKeeper requests only the permissions needed for app functionality. Permissions may vary depending on Android version and app features enabled.

Photo Access

If you choose to add a photo to a saved place, the app may use Android's photo picker or gallery access so you can select an image. The selected image may be copied into the app's local storage for display inside SpotKeeper.

Internet Access

Internet access may be used to open map links, display ads if monetization is enabled, or open external links such as the Play Store, website, or privacy policy page.

Network State

Network state access may be used to check whether internet is available before opening online features such as map links or ads.

Location Permission

SpotKeeper does not require background location tracking. The MVP version is designed to work without location permission by allowing users to manually paste map links.

5. Local Storage and Export/Import

SpotKeeper stores your saved place data locally on your device using local storage such as SQLite and internal app storage. Photos selected by you may be stored in app-specific storage.

If the app provides export or import features, you are responsible for where you save, share, upload, or transfer exported files. Exported files may contain your saved place details, so keep them private and secure.

Important: If you uninstall the app, clear app data, delete exported files, or delete photos, your saved information may be permanently removed unless you have created a backup.

6. Advertising

SpotKeeper may use Google AdMob or similar advertising services if ads are enabled in the app. Ads help support free app availability.

Advertising providers may collect or use certain device, app, or advertising-related information according to their own privacy policies. This may include information such as device identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location, or diagnostic data depending on your device settings and applicable laws.

SpotKeeper does not share your saved place names, personal notes, photos, or map links with advertising providers for ad targeting.

7. Third-Party Services and External Links

SpotKeeper may contain links or actions that open third-party services, such as Google Maps, Google Play Store, external websites, or advertising networks.

When you open a third-party service, your use of that service is governed by that third party's privacy policy and terms. Stone Forze is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services.

8. Children's Privacy

SpotKeeper is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app or related services, please contact us so we can review the issue.

9. Data Security

SpotKeeper is designed to keep your saved places locally on your device. However, no method of digital storage is completely secure. You should protect your device with a screen lock and avoid sharing exported backup files with people or services you do not trust.

Future versions may add optional privacy features such as PIN lock, biometric lock, or encrypted backups.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect app changes, legal requirements, or service updates. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SpotKeeper's privacy practices, you can contact Stone Forze through the official studio website.
