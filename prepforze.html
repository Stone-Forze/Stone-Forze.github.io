How ReviseX works

Simple setup, smart planning, and clear progress tracking for daily exam preparation.

1

Set up your exam

Add exam name, exam date, daily revision capacity, and preferred reminder time.

2

Add subjects and chapters

Organize your syllabus by subject, then add chapters with strength level, importance, notes, and revision time.

3

Follow today, tomorrow, and weekly plans

ReviseX generates a focused battle plan and moves missed chapters to the next available day.

4

Mark revised and track readiness

Mark chapters revised, update strength, save mock results, and watch your readiness score improve.

Revision planning logic

ReviseX creates plans using practical priority factors instead of random scheduling.

🔥

Priority Factors

Strength level, revision gap, exam urgency, mock score impact, and important chapter status are used to rank chapters.

🧠

Weak Chapter Focus

Weak and overdue chapters get higher priority so students revise the most important topics first.

🔁

Missed Chapter Handling

Missed chapters can move to tomorrow without overloading the day beyond the selected revision capacity.

Offline-first by design

Core ReviseX features work without internet. Students can open, plan, revise, and track progress anytime.

📦

Local Data Storage

Exam plans, subjects, chapters, mock tests, revision sessions, settings, and progress are stored locally on the device.

🚫

No Login Required

Version 1 does not require login, backend, Firebase, or cloud sync. The app remains simple and private.
