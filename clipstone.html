Our Core Philosophy

We believe that utility apps should be fast, battery-friendly, and completely respect your privacy. Modern smartphones are flooded with bloated applications that constantly sync data to the cloud. Clip Stone was built differently.

It is engineered from the ground up to be ultra-lightweight, ensuring smooth 60fps scrolling on every device—from entry-level phones to flagships—without draining your battery.

Is Your Data Secured?

Because a clipboard manager naturally handles highly sensitive information—such as private messages, addresses, and passwords—we engineered this app to ensure your data remains entirely under your control. The short answer is yes, your data is highly secure.

100% Offline Vault

Clip Stone does not have a backend server, cloud database, or remote backup system. Every piece of text is saved directly into a private Room database located purely on your device’s physical storage.

Android Sandbox Protection

Your history is locked inside the strict "App Sandbox" built into the Android OS. No other application on your phone can bypass the system to read Clip Stone’s internal database.

Zero Data Harvesting

We do not collect analytics on what you type or copy. We do not track your usage habits. We have zero visibility into your clipboard history.

Your Role in Security

Ensure your phone has a strong screen lock. We highly recommend manually swiping to delete highly sensitive items (like banking passwords) from your history after use.

Transparent Monetization

Transparency and user trust are our foundation. To keep the core features of the app entirely free for everyone, we use advertisements. Here is exactly how they work.

The Offline Paradox

Clip Stone is strictly offline, but the Google AdMob module requires internet to load banners. If you are offline, ads simply disappear. AdMob operates in an isolated sandbox and cannot read your private clipboard data.

Non-Intrusive Placements

We strictly limit ad placements. You will see an adaptive banner at the bottom of the screen, and occasional native cards within lists. We never use launch interstitials that block your core workflow.
