Is Your Data Secured?

Absolute privacy is the core foundation of Clip Stone. Because your clipboard naturally handles sensitive information like passwords, we engineered this app to ensure your data remains entirely under your control.

  • 100% Offline Vault: No backend servers. No cloud sync.
  • Android Sandbox: Locked via OS-level security.
  • Zero Harvesting: We have zero visibility into your history.

No Internet Required

Clip Stone operates entirely offline. Every piece of text you copy is saved directly into a local SQLite Room database located purely on your device’s physical storage. What happens on your device, stays on your device.

Transparent Monetization

How Ads Work

To keep the core features free, we use Google AdMob. If you are offline, ads simply disappear. AdMob operates in an isolated sandbox and cannot read, access, or scan your saved clipboard history.

Non-Intrusive Placements

We strictly limit ad placements to protect your workflow. You will see an adaptive banner at the bottom and occasional native cards in your list. We never use launch interstitials that block your immediate access to clips.
