Privacy Policy

Sundry - Precision Financial Ledger

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sundry ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your financial data remains secure and firmly under your control. This Privacy Policy details how we handle the information you provide when using our mobile application.

Because Sundry is designed as a precision, offline-first ledger, our philosophy is simple: Your data is yours.

2. Data Collection and Storage

Sundry operates primarily as an offline application. The financial data you enter into the app is stored locally on your physical device.

  • Local Storage: Transactions, budgets, groups, and receipt data are encrypted and stored within the app's secure internal database on your device.
  • No Cloud Syncing: We do not upload, store, or transmit your financial records, transaction history, or budget details to any external servers or databases owned by us.
  • Backups: You have the ability to manually export and import your database (e.g., as a local backup file or CSV). These backups are initiated by you and saved to a location of your choosing on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

While your core financial data remains local, Sundry integrates with a few carefully selected third-party services to enhance app functionality and support development:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain device information, such as your IP address, device identifiers (like the Android Advertising ID), and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can manage your ad preferences or opt-out of personalized ads via your device's Google Settings.
  • Google Play Services: The app utilizes the Google Play Core API to seamlessly check for app updates and request in-app reviews. This process communicates directly with the Google Play Store.
  • Currency Exchange Rates: When utilizing currency conversion features, the app makes secure, anonymous requests to public exchange rate APIs. No personal or transaction data is sent during these requests; only the currency codes are queried.

4. Permissions Required

To provide you with a premium experience, Sundry requires the following device permissions:

  • Camera: Used strictly for the Receipt Scanner feature to capture images of your physical receipts. Images are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Internet/Network Access: Required to fetch live currency exchange rates and to serve advertisements via Google AdMob.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain absolute control over its lifecycle.

  • You can delete individual transactions, budgets, or groups at any time within the app.
  • You can permanently delete all your data by navigating to the device Settings and clearing the app's data or by uninstalling Sundry entirely.

6. Children's Privacy

Sundry is a financial utility application intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please contact us so we can take immediate action to remove the information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
