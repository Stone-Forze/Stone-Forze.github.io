Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2024

At PrepForze, we believe that your focus should be on crushing your exams, not worrying about your data. This policy outlines how we handle information in our AI-powered preparation app.

TL;DR: We don't sell your data. Most of your study data stays on your device. We use industry-standard services like AdMob to keep the app free.

1. Information We Collect

PrepForze is designed to collect the minimum amount of data necessary to function:

  • Local Study Data: Your generated questions, test scores, and performance analytics are stored locally on your device using an encrypted database. We do not have access to your specific test results.
  • Device Information: Basic info like OS version and device model to ensure app stability.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide AI generation and keep the app free, we partner with:

  • Google AdMob: For serving ads. They may collect device identifiers to provide relevant ads and prevent fraud.
  • AI Providers (Gemini, Groq, NVIDIA): When you enter a topic for a quiz, that topic string is sent to these providers to generate your questions. No personal profile is attached to these requests.

3. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Internet: Required to generate AI questions and fetch ads.
  • Storage/Media: Only used if you choose to export your tests as PDFs to your Downloads folder.

4. Data Retention

Since your study data is local, it is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in your device settings. We do not maintain any cloud backups of your personal test history.

5. Children's Privacy

PrepForze is an educational tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.
