Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Clip Stone handles data. Clip Stone is designed as an offline-first clipboard manager that stores clipboard history locally on your device.

App NameClip Stone
Package Namecom.stoneforze.clipforze
DeveloperStoneforze
Last UpdatedMay 22, 2026

1. Information We Store

Clip Stone may store copied text that you save or that the app detects when you open or return to the app. This clipboard history is stored locally on your device using local database storage.

2. Local-First Data Handling

Your clipboard entries are intended to remain on your device. Clip Stone does not require an account, login, backend server, or cloud sync for its core clipboard features.

3. Clipboard Access

Due to Android privacy restrictions, Clip Stone does not continuously read your clipboard in the background. The app checks clipboard content when you open the app or return to it, and may save new copied text if it is different from the latest saved entry.

4. Share Intent

If you share selected text from another app to Clip Stone, the app may save that shared text locally so you can access it later.

5. Advertising

Clip Stone may use Google AdMob to show ads. Ads may require internet access and may use advertising-related identifiers or signals according to Google's advertising policies. If the device is offline, ad containers may be hidden or disabled.

6. Internet Usage

The core clipboard features work offline. Internet access may be used only for online features such as ads or future optional online functionality if added.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete individual clipboard entries or clear all history from inside the app. If an auto-delete option is enabled, older clips may be removed automatically after the selected time period.

8. Permissions

Clip Stone requests only permissions needed for app functionality. If internet permission is used, it is mainly for advertising or online features. Permission denial should be handled gracefully where applicable.

9. Children’s Privacy

Clip Stone is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since clipboard content is controlled by the user, users should avoid saving sensitive or private information they do not want stored locally.

10. Security

Clip Stone aims to keep clipboard data local and lightweight. However, users should avoid storing passwords, OTPs, financial information, or highly sensitive content in any clipboard manager.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features change. Updated versions should be published with a new last updated date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer or support channel provided by Stoneforze.
