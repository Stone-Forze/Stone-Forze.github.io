Section 1

Introduction & Brand Commitment

Welcome to Ownly – Personal Inventory Manager (“Ownly”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), an Android utility application designed to catalog, organize, track, and protect household belongings, home assets, warranties, and purchase receipts.

The application is developed and operated by Stoneforze. The official application identifier registered across Google Play and compiled throughout our codebase is:

com.stoneforze.ownly

Core Architecture: Offline-First Utility
Ownly operates on an offline-first foundation. By default, your inventory records, valuation amounts, serial numbers, condition notes, and receipts reside exclusively on your physical Android device. We do not sell or commercialize your household inventory data.

By downloading, installing, or continuing to use Ownly, you acknowledge the privacy and data handling practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with these practices, you may uninstall the application at any time.

Section 2

Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles. Ownly processes the following distinct categories of information based on your active usage:

A. Inventory and Household Belongings Data

  • Item Details: Item name, brand, model, serial number, description, notes, condition rating (New, Good, Fair, Damaged), and operational status (Active, Sold, Donated, Disposed, Archived).
  • Organization Context: Room assignments (Living Room, Kitchen, Bedroom, Office) and specific storage location container/bin labels.
  • Financial Values: Purchase date, purchase price, and preferred currency (e.g., USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, CAD, AUD).
  • Warranty Tracking: Warranty provider name, support contact, duration in months, expiration date, and alert reminder preferences.
  • Internal Identifiers: Randomly generated item UUIDs and QR pairing tokens.

Storage Scope: Stored locally in the application's on-device Room SQLite database. If cloud synchronization is optionally enabled with an authenticated Firebase user account, records are synchronized to Cloud Firestore under your private user path.

B. Photographs and Camera Images

  • Item & Receipt Photos: Selected by you using the privacy-safe Android Photo Picker. Images are decoded, EXIF rotation-corrected, scaled (max 1920×1080), and compressed to JPEG format on-device by ImageCompressor.java.
  • Private Sandbox Storage: Compressed images and 300px thumbnails are saved strictly within Ownly's internal, private app storage directory (context.getFilesDir()).
  • Cloud Storage Scope: In the current production release, inventory photographs remain exclusively on your device. They are not uploaded to remote cloud buckets (Firebase Storage is not active in the application code).

C. Purchase Receipts and Documents

Stored locally in internal application storage and included only when you explicitly generate an Insurance Inventory Schedule PDF or CSV report and share it through Android's native share sheet.

D. QR Code Scanning & Processing

  • Camera Scanner: Uses CameraX and ZXing on-device to decode QR barcodes in real time. Video frames are processed in volatile memory and discarded immediately; no camera feed or video is ever recorded or uploaded.
  • Gallery Image Scanner: When selecting a QR image from your gallery, QrImageDecoder.java opens the content URI, downsamples the bitmap, runs multi-pass ZXing binarizers, and recycles memory. Decoded strings are never sent to external servers.

E. Technical, Diagnostic, and Ad-Serving Data

Collected automatically by integrated third-party SDKs:

  • Device Attributes: Device model, manufacturer, OS version (API level), system language, and screen dimensions.
  • Network Information: IP address (used by Google AdMob and Firebase to derive coarse geographic location and combat ad fraud).
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), subject to your Android privacy settings.
  • Crash Logs: Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymous stack traces and device memory states during unexpected app crashes.

Section 4

Local Storage & Firebase Synchronization

A. Local On-Device Storage (Android Jetpack Room)

Ownly's primary operational database is built with Android Jetpack Room:

  • Database Path: /data/data/com.stoneforze.ownly/databases/ownly_database.
  • Files Path: Photographs, thumbnails, and generated PDF/CSV reports reside in /data/data/com.stoneforze.ownly/files/.
  • Sandboxed Isolation: Under Android's Linux kernel sandbox, no third-party app installed on your device can access or read Ownly's database or private files.
  • Complete Offline Operation: You can create, edit, browse, search, and export your entire inventory without an active internet connection.

B. Cloud Synchronization (Google Firebase)

  • Gated Authentication: Synchronization occurs only if you explicitly sign in with a Firebase user account. When unauthenticated, sync is bypassed completely.
  • Sync Architecture: When authenticated, structured text records are queued locally in a sync_queue table and synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore over TLS/HTTPS encrypted connections using Android WorkManager.
  • Security Rules: Firestore security rules strictly isolate records under users/{userId}, preventing cross-user data leakage.
  • Google Firebase Privacy: For infrastructure security details, see Firebase Privacy & Security.
Section 5

Advertising & Google AdMob

Ownly is distributed as a free mobile inventory tool supported by in-app advertising delivered through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob).

A. Ad Formats Implemented in Ownly

  • Banner Ads: Adaptive banners at the bottom of utility screens (Search, Rooms).
  • Native Advanced Ads: In-feed sponsored cards styled with Quantum Palette tokens to blend naturally with item lists.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen transition ads presented only after completing significant actions (e.g., adding an item). Strictly capped at a maximum of 3 impressions per session with a 5-minute cooldown.
  • Rewarded Ads: User-initiated video ads to unlock special entitlements (such as the 24-hour Enhanced Insurance Audit Schedule).
  • App Open Ads: Displayed when reopening Ownly from the background, suppressed during splash screen, onboarding, and active QR camera scanning, with a 4-hour freshness cache rule.

B. Information Processed by Google AdMob

Google AdMob may process your Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address (for coarse geographic targeting and fraud detection), app package identifier (com.stoneforze.ownly), and ad interaction telemetry.

C. User Consent and Privacy Controls (Google UMP)

  • EEA and UK Compliance: Integrated with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to gather user consent in compliance with GDPR and IAB TCF v2.2 before requesting personalized ads.
  • Manage Anytime: You can review or modify your ad consent choices at any time inside Ownly via Settings > Ad Privacy & Consent Choices.
  • Device OS Controls: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID via Android Settings: Settings > Google > Ads.
Zero Inventory Sharing with Advertisers:
Ownly does not share your item names, purchase amounts, serial numbers, photographs, or household locations with Google AdMob or any ad network.

Section 7

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, commercialize, or trade your personal inventory details, photographs, or financial records. Data sharing occurs exclusively under these specific circumstances:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: When cloud sync is active, records are processed by Google Firebase servers pursuant to standard contractual clauses and cloud security certifications.
  • Monetization Partners: Google AdMob receives anonymized technical device identifiers and ad telemetry strictly to display advertisements and prevent fraudulent traffic.
  • User-Initiated Sharing: When you generate an Insurance Inventory PDF or CSV schedule, you explicitly choose the destination app (email, messaging, Drive) via Android's native share sheet.
  • Legal Mandates: Where required by valid court order, warrant, subpoena, or applicable statutory law within a competent jurisdiction.
Section 8

Data Retention & Deletion

A. Local Device Data Retention

  • Continuous Persistence: All inventory records, rooms, warranties, photographs, and receipts remain on your device until you delete them within the app or clear application storage.
  • Immediate Local Erase: Clear all local data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Ownly > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling Ownly from your device immediately deletes the local sandbox directory, including the Room SQLite database, photos, and receipts.

B. Cloud Data Retention & Deletion Requests

  • Independent Cloud Lifecycle: Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete data previously synchronized to Cloud Firestore.
  • Submitting a Deletion Request: Send an email to our privacy desk at stoneforze.help@gmail.com with the subject line OWNLY DATA DELETION REQUEST. Please include your registered account email or your unique Firebase UID.
  • Execution Timeline: Verified deletion requests are executed within 30 calendar days, permanently removing all Firestore documents under your user account from active clusters.
Section 9

Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security controls:

  • Android Application Sandboxing: Ownly operates in an isolated Linux sandbox. Local database files and images are readable only by Ownly's unique Linux UID.
  • Encrypted Communications: All network exchanges with Google Firebase and Google AdMob use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Cloud Security Rules: Cloud Firestore declarative security rules enforce access tokens, restricting document read/write access solely to the authenticated owner.
  • Secure File Sharing: Exported reports and receipts are shared exclusively through Android's secure FileProvider architecture (com.stoneforze.ownly.fileprovider) with short-lived, read-only URI grants.

Security Note: No transmission over the internet or mobile storage medium is 100% impenetrable against physical device compromise or advanced unauthorized intrusion.

Permissions & Device Access

Ownly requests only the minimal set of Android system permissions strictly necessary to fulfill advertised features:

Declared Android Permission Type Purpose in Ownly User Revocation
android.permission.CAMERA Runtime Used strictly during the QR Scanner activity to scan item barcodes and storage QR codes. Never records video. Prompted on first scan; revocable via Android Settings.
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Enables Cloud Firestore synchronization, UMP consent retrieval, and Google AdMob ad loading. Granted automatically by Android at installation.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Checks whether Wi-Fi or cellular network exists before initiating cloud sync or ad preloads. Granted automatically by Android at installation.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime Delivers local scheduled alerts for upcoming warranty expirations on Android 13+ (API 33+). Prompted at runtime; can be toggled in App Settings.
android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Special Triggers warranty expiration notifications at the exact scheduled calendar date and time. Manageable under Special App Access settings.
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal Provides subtle haptic feedback on successful QR scan detection and critical warranty notifications. Granted automatically; follows device vibration mode.
Photo Gallery Privacy Assurance:
Ownly does not request broad filesystem permissions (such as legacy READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). We utilize Android's official system Photo Picker, ensuring Ownly receives access only to the specific image files you deliberately choose.
