1. Developer Information

This Privacy Policy explains how STONE FORZE ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you play Arrow Drift.

2. What Data Arrow Drift Stores

Arrow Drift stores certain gameplay progression and settings locally on your device so that your progress and preferences can be maintained between sessions. This includes:

  • Current and completed levels
  • In-game currency (Scratch cards, lives, skips, hints)
  • Purchased and unlocked cosmetic skins and themes
  • Audio and haptic preferences
  • Tutorial progress
  • Ad frequency and cooldown information

This core progression data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to a central game server.

3. Advertising Data

To support the development of Arrow Drift, we use third-party advertising services (Google AdMob), which includes Interstitial, Rewarded, App Open, and Banner ads. These third-party services may process information for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and related purposes.

The Google AdMob SDK may collect and share:

  • IP addresses
  • User product interactions
  • Diagnostic information
  • Device/account identifiers
  • Advertising ID / App Set ID (where applicable)

4. Personalized Advertising & Consent

Depending on your location and applicable laws (such as GDPR in Europe), advertising partners may request your consent before personalized advertising is provided. Users may be able to manage applicable advertising and privacy choices through the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent mechanism provided in the app, or via their device settings.

5. Rewarded Advertisements

Arrow Drift offers optional rewarded advertisements (e.g., Watch Ad to refill lives or earn scratch cards). If you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, the app may provide an in-game reward after the advertising provider confirms that the required viewing event has been completed. The app does not provide the reward when the advertisement fails to load or is not successfully completed.

6. Diagnostics & Analytics

Arrow Drift utilizes Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to monitor game stability, track crash reports, and analyze anonymous gameplay trends to help us improve the app. This data is handled according to Google's standard analytics privacy practices.

7. Purchases

In-app purchases, where available, are processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. Arrow Drift does not receive, process, or store your full payment card details or any other complete payment credentials.

8. No Account / No Login

Arrow Drift does not currently require you to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to play the game. However, please note that device-related information may still be processed by the aforementioned advertising and analytics SDKs.

9. Internet Usage

Core gameplay and locally stored progression are designed to remain available without an internet connection. Internet connectivity is only required for third-party advertising, analytics, and in-app purchases. If an online service is unavailable, you can still enjoy the core puzzle offline without interruption.

10. Data Security (How We Protect Your Data)

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect the information handled by the app against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes utilizing Android's secure encrypted preferences, safe local storage practices, defensive data validation, and ensuring network communications rely on HTTPS/TLS via our integrated SDKs.

11. Data Retention

Gameplay progression and preferences remain securely stored on your device until they are manually deleted by you, the app is uninstalled, or the app's data is cleared from your system settings. Third-party service providers (like AdMob and Firebase) retain information according to their own applicable privacy policies and retention practices.

12. Data Deletion

You can instantly remove all locally stored Arrow Drift data and progression at any time by using your Android device's app-data clearing controls (Settings > Apps > Arrow Drift > Storage > Clear Data) or simply by uninstalling the application.

13. Children's Privacy

Arrow Drift is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information outside the scope of standard SDK operations, please contact us.
