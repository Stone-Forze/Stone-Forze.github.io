Privacy Policy

SafariSprout: Kids Learning Games · Developed by StoneForze
Last updated: November 1, 2026

Our Commitment to Children's Privacy

SafariSprout is an educational application designed for young learners, including children aged 2–8. No account or registration is required to use its core learning features. Learning progress and preferences are stored locally on the device. The application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, which may process certain technical and advertising-related information.

1. Information We Collect

SafariSprout does not require children to create accounts or submit personal information to use its core educational features.

We do not intentionally collect the following directly through the application:

  • Children's names or personal profiles.
  • Email addresses or phone numbers.
  • Home addresses.
  • Photographs or videos.
  • Microphone recordings.
  • Precise GPS location.

Automatically Processed Information

Because SafariSprout uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), certain technical information may be processed by Google and its advertising services.

Depending on the device, SDK configuration, and applicable advertising requirements, this may include:

  • IP address and information derived from it, such as approximate location.
  • Device identifiers and advertising-related identifiers.
  • App and device information.
  • Advertising interactions and delivery information.
  • Diagnostics, performance information, and information used to detect invalid advertising activity.

This information may be processed by Google to provide advertising services, maintain service security, prevent fraud, and perform other purposes described in Google's privacy documentation.

StoneForze does not directly operate Google's advertising infrastructure and does not independently control all information processed by Google.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

SafariSprout stores certain app information locally to support its educational features.

  • Learning progress.
  • Completed activities.
  • Stars, badges, and achievements.
  • Sound preferences.
  • Voice and narration settings.
  • Other application preferences.

This information is stored in the application's local storage and is not intentionally uploaded to a StoneForze server.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing SafariSprout's app storage through your Android device settings or uninstalling the application.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising — Google AdMob

SafariSprout uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements and support the development and maintenance of the application.

Google may process certain technical and advertising-related information as described in its privacy policies.

Advertising and Children's Privacy

Because SafariSprout is designed for children, we intend to use advertising configurations appropriate for child-directed applications.

Our advertising implementation is intended to:

  • Request child-directed advertising treatment where applicable.
  • Restrict advertising content to an appropriate general audience rating.
  • Avoid personalized advertising and behavioral advertising directed at children.
  • Avoid using children's personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Keep advertisements separate from core educational interactions.

Advertising configuration and eligibility are subject to Google's current policies and applicable legal requirements.

Advertising Placement

Advertisements may appear in designated advertising areas of the application.

We aim to ensure that advertisements do not unnecessarily interrupt educational activities, tracing exercises, or interactive learning experiences.

Advertising availability and formats may depend on device connectivity, advertising inventory, and Google's policies.

Information Processed by Google

Google's advertising services may process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Device and advertising identifiers.
  • Advertising request and impression information.
  • Ad interactions.
  • App diagnostics and performance information.
  • Information required for fraud prevention and service security.

The precise information processed depends on Google's services, the SDK version, device configuration, and applicable privacy settings.

Google's Privacy Documentation

For additional information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps and websites
  • Google AdMob Policies

4. Child Safety and Parental Controls

SafariSprout is designed with young learners and their families in mind.

We aim to:

  • Keep the learning experience simple and child-friendly.
  • Avoid requiring children to register or create accounts.
  • Protect designated external links and parent configuration areas using a Parental Gate.
  • Avoid intentionally collecting children's personal information.
  • Configure advertising services in accordance with applicable child-directed advertising requirements.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the application and any external services accessed through it.

The Parental Gate is an access-control feature. It should not be interpreted as a substitute for legally required parental consent.

5. Internet Connectivity

Core educational functionality is designed to work offline wherever the relevant content and resources are available locally.

An internet connection may be required for:

  • Loading advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Other online services explicitly provided by the application.

When an internet connection is unavailable, advertisements may not load. This should not prevent access to the application's available offline learning features.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Providers

StoneForze does not sell children's personal information.

We use Google AdMob as a third-party advertising service. Information processed through AdMob is handled by Google in accordance with its applicable privacy policies and service terms.

We do not independently provide children's personal information to advertisers for behavioral profiling.

Third-party services may process information as necessary to provide their services, maintain security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Application Data

Learning progress and preferences stored locally remain on the device until they are cleared or the application is uninstalled.

To delete local SafariSprout data:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps.
  3. Select SafariSprout.
  4. Open Storage.
  5. Select Clear Data or Clear Storage.

Alternatively, uninstall SafariSprout.

Advertising-Related Data

Advertising-related information processed by Google is subject to Google's data retention, deletion, and privacy practices. StoneForze does not control Google's independent retention systems.

For more information, visit the Google Privacy Policy .
