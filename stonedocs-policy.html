Privacy Policy

Updated Policy

Your Privacy Matters.

StoneDocs is designed as an offline-first productivity application. We prioritize user privacy, minimal data collection, secure document handling, and transparent policies.

1. Information We Collect

StoneDocs does not require account creation or mandatory sign-in. Most document processing happens locally on your device.

  • Basic app analytics (if enabled)
  • Crash reports for app stability
  • Advertising identifiers for AdMob ads
  • Local file access only when granted by user

2. File Access & Permissions

StoneDocs requests only the permissions required for app functionality.

  • Storage access for opening and saving documents
  • Camera access for document scanning
  • Internet access for ads and optional online services

3. Offline-First Processing

Most PDF editing, viewing, scanning, and conversion features work completely offline. Your files remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export them.

4. Advertising

StoneDocs uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Ads may use advertising identifiers to improve ad relevance.

  • Native ads
  • Interstitial ads
  • Rewarded video ads

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect local processing and app integrity. However, no digital platform can guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Services

StoneDocs may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services
  • ML Kit

7. Children's Privacy

StoneDocs is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes become effective immediately after being posted on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or support, contact:

support@stonedocs.app
