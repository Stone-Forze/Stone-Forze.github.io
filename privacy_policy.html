Privacy Policy for HerdKeeper

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

Welcome to HerdKeeper ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use the HerdKeeper mobile application (the "App").

HerdKeeper is designed as an offline-first animal management application. We prioritize your privacy by keeping your data on your own device.

1. Data Collection and Storage

Local Storage: All user-generated data—including animal profiles, health records, timelines, financial records, and settings—is stored locally on your device using SQLite databases and local file storage. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not transmit, sync, or store this data on our servers. Your data belongs to you.

2. Permissions We Request

To provide core functionalities, the App requests the following permissions:

  • Camera and Photo Library (Storage): We request these permissions so you can take or select photos to attach to your animal profiles and gallery. Photos you select or take are copied directly into the App's secure local storage on your device. We never upload, view, or share your photos.
  • Notifications: We request notification permissions to provide you with local, on-device reminders for animal health events, breeding, vaccinations, and custom alerts. These notifications are scheduled locally and do not rely on a cloud server.

3. Third-Party Services

While the core App operates completely offline, we use third-party services to support the App's development (such as serving advertisements).

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and use certain data (such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data) to provide personalized and non-personalized ads.
    For more information on how Google uses data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Backup and Restore

The App includes a feature that allows you to manually create a backup of your data (the `.hkbak` file). When you trigger a backup, a compressed archive of your local database and images is created on your device. You can choose to share or save this file to third-party services (like Google Drive or email) at your own discretion. We do not have access to these backups.

5. Data Security

We rely on the standard security mechanisms provided by the Android operating system to protect the data stored within the App's secure sandbox. Since we do not transmit your personal animal records over the internet, the risk of data interception by us or third parties is eliminated by design. However, you are responsible for keeping your device physically secure and utilizing device-level security (like screen locks).

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
