Privacy Policy & Terms


SpotKeeper ("the App") is developed by Stone Forze. This application is designed to function as a standalone utility.


Data Stewardship

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your location data. All "Spots" you save are encrypted and stored within the App's private directory. The location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) is invoked only during active use to pin your current coordinates.


Monetization

To keep the project sustainable, we utilize Google AdMob. Third-party advertisers may process device identifiers for personalized ad delivery. Please consult the AdMob privacy statement for details.
