Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

1. Data Collection

SpotKeeper is built with privacy in mind. We do not collect your personal data or save your places on any external server. All coordinates, notes, and photos stay locally on your device.

2. Location Permissions

Location access is used only to show your current position on the map and calculate distances to your saved spots. This data is never shared with us or third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob for monetization. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more details.

4. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally, you are responsible for its safety. You can use the "Export" feature in settings to create backups of your saved places.
