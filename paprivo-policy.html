01.

Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Paprivo: PDF Reader & Tools (referred to as "Paprivo", "the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). Paprivo is a native Android application built to provide offline-first PDF viewing, document creation, image conversion, page management, and annotation utilities.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the handling of information when you install, access, or use Paprivo. The core architecture of Paprivo is designed so that your files, documents, and creative inputs are processed locally on your Android device. We do not operate remote document-processing servers, we do not require user accounts or logins, and we do not transmit your document contents to cloud storage.

Summary: Paprivo works offline for all document tasks. Network connectivity is utilized exclusively by third-party advertising SDKs (Google AdMob) and user consent management (Google User Messaging Platform) to deliver advertising and fraud prevention.

02.

Information We Collect

We categorize the information handled in connection with Paprivo into the following distinct categories:

A. User Documents and Media Files

  • PDF Documents: Files you explicitly pick or open from device storage, file managers, or third-party applications via standard Android intents.
  • Image Files: Photographs or images you select from your device gallery or photo picker for conversion into PDF documents.
  • Processed Outputs: New files generated at your direction (such as merged PDFs, split page ranges, compressed PDFs, rotated documents, or newly created notes).
  • Markups & Annotations: Freehand pen strokes, highlighter paths, and colors you draw on document pages.

Crucial Privacy Principle: All document contents, text, images, and drawings remain strictly local on your device. Paprivo does not upload, duplicate to remote servers, read for profiling, or expose your documents to external networks.

B. Local Application Data & Preferences

To provide continuity across reading sessions, Paprivo saves operational metadata locally on your device using Android's private Room SQLite database and SharedPreferences:

  • Document Library History: Document URI references, filenames, file sizes, page counts, last-read page numbers, and timestamps.
  • Favorites & Bookmarks: Specific documents you mark as favorites or individual page indices you bookmark.
  • Annotation Geometry: Normalized vector coordinates of pen and highlighter markings tied to specific document URIs and page indices.
  • User Preferences: Theme selection (Light / Dark / System Default), document sorting preferences (by Name, Date, Size, Ascending/Descending), reader zoom defaults, page scroll modes (Paged swipe vs. Continuous scroll), and page transition animation styles.

This data is stored entirely in Android's app-private sandbox storage and is deleted automatically if you clear application data or uninstall the app.

C. Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob & UMP)

Paprivo integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to support free access to the application through advertisements.

Depending on your regional location, consent choices, and Android device settings, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically process:

  • Device & Hardware Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen dimensions, language, and carrier.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), subject to your Android OS privacy settings and consent declarations.
  • Network Information: Coarse IP address (used by Google for general geographic estimation, fraud prevention, and regional ad compliance).
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, viewability, and diagnostic signals.

Note: The developer of Paprivo does not collect, sell, or receive personal identity records from AdMob. All advertising data collection is governed directly by Google's Privacy Policy.

D. Information You Voluntarily Provide

Paprivo does not include user registration, profiles, or login screens. If you contact our support or developer contact email directly, we receive your email address, name (if attached to your email client), and whatever details you include in your message. This information is used strictly to assist with your technical inquiries.

04.

On-Device Document Processing Model

Paprivo’s core document engine is built on Android’s native PdfRenderer API and local open-source libraries (Apache PDFBox for Android).

  • Zero Remote Uploads: When you open a PDF, convert images, merge files, or rotate pages, the byte processing happens entirely within your device's memory (RAM and CPU/GPU).
  • Storage Access Framework (SAF): Paprivo interacts with documents through Android’s secure Storage Access Framework. The application requests access only to the specific files or folders you authorize through the Android system file picker.
  • Temporary & Cache Data: During rendering, bitmap thumbnails and page caches are created in volatile memory or the app's private cache directory (context.cacheDir). These temporary caches are trimmed automatically during low-memory conditions and can be purged at any time by clearing the app cache.
  • Non-Destructive Operations: Editing operations (such as rotating, splitting, or merging) generate a fresh output file in your designated storage location. Your original source files remain intact and unmodified unless you explicitly delete them using a file manager.

Android Permissions & File Access

Paprivo declares only the permissions strictly required to deliver its documented functionality. We do not request broad device administration or unrestricted access to personal folders.

Declared Permission Type Specific Purpose in Paprivo
INTERNET Normal Permission Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform to request and display advertisements and update consent statuses. Not used for document transmission.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Permission Allows the advertising framework to check whether an active internet connection is available before requesting ad content.
CAMERA Runtime Permission Optional. Used only if you choose to capture physical documents or photographs directly with your camera for conversion to PDF. Declared with android.hardware.camera required="false" so devices without cameras can still install the app.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Runtime Permission (API 33+) Enables the photo picker to display and select images from your gallery for the Image-to-PDF conversion tool.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Runtime Permission (API ≤ 32) Legacy storage permission for Android 12 and earlier, enabling user-directed selection of PDF files and images.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime Permission (API 33+) Allows Paprivo to notify you when long-running file operations (such as large PDF merges or batch exports) have successfully finished.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Normal Permission Used solely to refresh Paprivo's home screen widget ("Recent Documents") when your device boots up.

You may grant or revoke optional runtime permissions (such as Camera) at any time through your device's Settings > Apps > Paprivo > Permissions without affecting core PDF viewing capabilities.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We adhere to strict data-minimization practices regarding sharing and disclosure:

  • Document Content Sharing: We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer your document contents, file paths, images, or annotations to any third party.
  • Advertising Partners: Google AdMob operates as an independent data controller with respect to ad serving and fraud mitigation data collected through its SDK, as governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • System Sharing via Android Intents: When you use Paprivo's "Share PDF", "Print", or "Open With" features, document files are shared strictly with the destination application you choose (such as your email client, cloud drive, or printer service) through Android's secure FileProvider.
  • Legal Compulsion: Because we do not transmit or store your documents or account credentials on remote servers, we have no user document repositories to provide to third parties or law enforcement agencies.
08.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because Paprivo operates primarily on your device, you have direct control over data retention:

  • User Documents: Documents you create or save (such as merged PDFs or exports) remain in your device’s storage until you delete them using a file manager or storage tool. Removing a document from Paprivo’s "Recent" library list simply removes the database reference; it does not delete the physical file from your device.
  • In-App Database Records: Document history, favorites, bookmarks, and markup coordinates are stored in the app’s internal Room database. You can clear this at any time by going to Settings > Apps > Paprivo > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Temporary Bitmaps: Rendered page previews and thumbnail caches in context.cacheDir are purged when system memory is low and can be cleared via Clear Cache in Android Settings.
  • Application Uninstallation: Uninstalling Paprivo immediately and permanently removes all internal database records, preferences, and cached thumbnails from your device. Files stored in public storage (such as your Downloads folder) remain under your file manager's control.
09.

Security Safeguards & Architecture

We employ robust mobile engineering safeguards to protect your document privacy:

  • Android Application Sandbox: Paprivo runs in a dedicated Android process with a unique Linux user ID, preventing other non-root applications from accessing its private database or temporary rendering files.
  • Secure File Sharing: When sharing or exporting documents, Paprivo uses androidx.core.content.FileProvider with short-lived, granular URI permissions (FLAG_GRANT_READ_URI_PERMISSION), eliminating insecure exposed filesystem paths.
  • Local Isolation: By avoiding cloud processing pipelines, your documents are shielded from network interception, server data breaches, and remote configuration errors.

Security Notice: While on-device processing minimizes external exposure, the security of files stored on your device depends on your device's security configuration (screen lock PIN/biometrics, encryption status, and avoidance of rooted or untrusted operating systems).
