Your Privacy Matters.

ReviseX is designed with a privacy-first approach. Your study plans, revision progress, subjects, chapters, and mock tests remain stored locally on your device. We do not require account creation or cloud storage in this version.

📅 Last Updated: 16 May 2026
🔒

Data Collection & Storage

ReviseX is built as an offline-first Android application. Your study-related data is stored locally on your device using secure local storage technologies.

  • Exam plans and schedules
  • Subjects and chapters
  • Revision progress and streaks
  • Mock test reminders and results
  • Theme and reminder preferences

ReviseX does not require login and does not upload your personal study data to external servers in Version 1.

📢

Advertising & AdMob

ReviseX may use Google AdMob to display lightweight advertisements inside the application.

  • Banner ads
  • Native ads
  • Limited interstitial ads

Google AdMob may collect device identifiers or usage-related information to provide relevant advertisements and improve ad performance.

Users can manage ad personalization preferences directly through Android or Google account privacy settings.

🛡️

Permissions

ReviseX only requests permissions necessary for core app features.

  • Notification Permission — used for daily revision reminders, mock test reminders, and weekly study notifications.

ReviseX does not request access to:

  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Microphone
  • Camera
  • SMS
  • Call logs
⚡

Data Security

ReviseX is designed with modern Android best practices to protect local app data.

  • Local-only data storage
  • No unnecessary permissions
  • No forced account creation
  • No background tracking services
  • No hidden data collection
🎓

Children & Student Safety

ReviseX is intended for students and learners. We aim to provide a safe and focused study environment with minimal distractions and privacy-first functionality.

Users are encouraged to review this page occasionally for updates.
