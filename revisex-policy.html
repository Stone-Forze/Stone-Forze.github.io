Privacy Policy

We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve personalized ads. You can disable personalization in your device settings.

ReviseX requests notification permissions to send you daily study reminders and mock test alerts. We do not access your contacts, location, or gallery.
