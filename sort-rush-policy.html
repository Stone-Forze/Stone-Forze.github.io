1. Information We Collect

STONE FORZE respects your privacy. Sort Rush: Waste Sorting Game does not directly collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Microphone or audio recordings
  • User account credentials

2. Game Data Stored Locally

To provide you with a seamless gaming experience, certain game progression data is stored locally directly on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers. Locally stored data includes:

  • Level progress and unlocked worlds
  • High scores and total sorts
  • In-game currency (Coins)
  • Inventory (Power-ups)
  • Settings and preferences (Sound, Haptics, Colorblind mode, etc.)
  • Daily reward progress and streaks

3. Advertising

Our game is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob. While the game itself does not collect personal data, third-party advertising SDKs integrated into the app can process data to serve ads.

AdMob and Google may process information such as advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, device/app information, and ad interaction information for the purposes of advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention, according to the applicable Google and AdMob privacy policies. For more information on how Google uses data, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Analytics / Crash Reporting

Sort Rush does not currently use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services (such as Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics). We do not track your gameplay metrics remotely.

5. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service currently integrated into Sort Rush is:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)

6. Internet Connection

The core gameplay of Sort Rush can be played entirely offline without an internet connection. However, an internet connection may be required to load and display advertisements and to access other potential online services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Because all core game data is saved locally on your device, it is protected by your device's built-in security mechanisms (such as screen locks and device encryption). We do not maintain servers that store your personal information.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally Stored Data: Your game data remains on your device indefinitely until you clear the app's data through your device settings or uninstall the app, subject to Android/device behavior. Deleting the app will permanently delete your progression and coins.

Third-Party Services: Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own retention policies.

9. Children's Privacy

We are committed to protecting the privacy of children. If Sort Rush is part of the Google Play Families program or is directed at an audience that includes children under 13, we ensure that our advertising practices comply with Google Play's Families Policy requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child's privacy, please contact us.

10. Your Rights / Choices

As a user, you have control over your data:

  • Clear Local Data: You can wipe all your local game data at any time by going to your device's Settings > Apps > Sort Rush > Storage > Clear Data. (Note: This will reset all your game progress).
  • Advertising Personalization: You can disable or reset advertising personalization through your device settings (e.g., Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization).
  • Contact Us: You can contact the developer at any time regarding privacy questions.
