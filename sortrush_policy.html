SORT RUSH — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: August 16, 2026
Last Updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sort Rush (“Sort Rush,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Sort Rush mobile application (the “App”).

Sort Rush is a casual single-player waste-sorting game developed and published under StoneForze.

By downloading or using Sort Rush, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We designed Sort Rush to minimize the information that we collect directly from users.

1.1 Information You Provide

Sort Rush does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or password to play the game.

We do not intentionally request personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Microphone recordings
  • Camera data
  • Precise location
  • Health information
  • Financial information
  • Social media account information

unless a future version of the App introduces a feature that specifically requires such information. If this occurs, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

2. Game Data Stored on Your Device

Sort Rush stores certain gameplay and preference information locally on your device so that the game can remember your progress.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information may include:

  • Level progress
  • Completed levels
  • Stars earned
  • High scores
  • Best combo
  • Coins
  • Shop purchases
  • Daily reward status
  • Daily challenge progress
  • Game settings
  • Music preferences
  • Sound-effect preferences
  • Haptic preferences
  • Notification preferences
  • Other gameplay-related settings

This information is primarily stored using local device storage such as SharedPreferences or equivalent local storage mechanisms.

We do not require you to create an online account to store this gameplay information.

If you uninstall the App, locally stored game data may be deleted by the operating system. We do not guarantee that locally stored progress can be recovered after uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data.
