About ReviseX

ReviseX is built to help students stop guessing what to study next.

🎯

Core purpose

ReviseX creates a simple exam revision battle plan based on subjects, chapters, weak areas, mock tests, and progress.

🔒

Offline-first

The app is designed to work offline. Study plans, subjects, chapters, reminders, and progress are stored locally on the device.
